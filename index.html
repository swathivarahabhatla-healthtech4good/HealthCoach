💊 Thyroid timing rules

  • Take the pill on waking with water only, same time every day.
  • Wait 30–60 min before breakfast, coffee or tea.
  • Keep calcium, iron, soy and high-fiber supplements 4 h away from the pill.
  • Eat consistently — big changes in fiber or soy can change how much medicine you absorb.
  • Get TSH checked as your doctor advises, especially after weight change.

General guidance only, not medical advice. Check diet and supplement changes with your doctor or dietitian.
